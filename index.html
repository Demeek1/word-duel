❔ How to play

🔤 You get a word, like BOOK.

✏️ Change exactly one letter to make a new real word of the same length — BOOK → TOOK.

🔁 In Duel, the other player continues from your word. In Solo & Daily, keep the chain going yourself.

⏱️ Each turn's clock shrinks as you go, down to 5 seconds.

🚫 No repeating a word already used.

⭐ Scoring: each word = 10 base, +5 per extra letter, plus a speed bonus for answering fast — all multiplied by your combo as the chain grows. You'll see the points pop up on every word!
