🔤 One-Letter Word Duel

Two players. Change one letter. Beat the clock.

How to play:
• You're given a word (e.g. BOOK).
• Change exactly one letter to make a new real word — same length (BOOK → TOOK).
• The other player continues from your word.
• Each turn's clock shrinks as the round goes on, down to a 5-second floor.
• No repeating a word already used. Run out of time and you lose the round — first to the match target wins!
